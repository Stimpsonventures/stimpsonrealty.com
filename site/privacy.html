Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Stimpson Realty, affiliated with Silvercreek Realty Group, respects your privacy. This notice describes the information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you request a buyer's guide, submit a contact or valuation inquiry, or subscribe to market updates, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, property information, message, and the source of your request.

How we use it

We use this information to fulfill your requests, deliver requested materials, respond to inquiries, provide requested market updates, and maintain records of client communications. We do not sell your contact information.

Services and sharing

Our website may use Netlify for hosting and form processing, an email delivery provider for transactional messages, and a real estate CRM for managing inquiries when connected. These providers may process information on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Marketing choices

Requesting a buyer's guide or property valuation does not automatically enroll you in recurring marketing emails. Market updates require separate consent; you may unsubscribe using instructions in any marketing message or by contacting us.

Website data and third-party links

Our hosting providers may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs for security and performance. Links to Google and Navica lead to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. Property search functionality may use local browser storage for preferences and visit counts when implemented.

Data access and retention

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable record-retention requirements. We retain inquiry information as needed for customer service, operational records, and legal obligations.
